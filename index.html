What the app will do

Budget Intelligence

The app will adapt recommendations to a user’s budget or generate low-cost, balanced, and premium options.

Personalized Itineraries

Users will receive day-by-day travel plans based on preferences, trip duration, travel style, and interests.

Location Awareness

The project will consider nearby amenities such as restaurants, transportation, pharmacies, attractions, and safe zones.
